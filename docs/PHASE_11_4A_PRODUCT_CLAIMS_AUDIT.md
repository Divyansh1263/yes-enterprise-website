## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Methodology

This evidence-based audit rigorously evaluates every marketing, material, design, and performance claim present in the product descriptions, specifications, tags, and titles across the 19 WhatsApp catalogue items.

In accordance with strict legal, consumer-protection, and e-commerce compliance guidelines, claims are classified into five distinct evidentiary categories:
1. **Visible Feature:** Directly observable and verifiable in high-resolution photographs (markings, engraved logos, physical gaskets, valves).
2. **Documented Material Claim:** Supported by formal metallurgical, polymer, or laboratory certificates (e.g., mill test reports, FDA/BIS test certs).
3. **Manufacturer Claim:** Stated by the manufacturer/seller in WhatsApp text, retail box printing, or sales sheets, but lacking independent third-party laboratory verification.
4. **Performance Claim:** Involves functional thresholds over time, pressure, or temperature (e.g., thermal retention hours, leak-proof limits).
5. **Unverified:** Claims lacking direct visual proof, documentation, or seller corroboration.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Core Principle:** Physical markings or visible features (such as a laser-etched "SS 304" or a silicone O-ring) confirm the *presence of the mark or feature*, not laboratory proof of chemical composition or absolute performance. Unverified performance claims must not be published as unqualified factual guarantees without owner sign-off.

## 4. Recommendations for Safe Catalogue Publishing

1. **Avoid Absolute Guarantees:** Rephrase "100% Leak-Proof" to *"Engineered with airtight silicone sealing gasket and secure snap-lock levers to prevent spills."*
2. **Qualify Thermal Claims:** Rephrase "Keeps liquids hot or cold for 24 hours" to *"Double-wall vacuum insulation designed for prolonged temperature retention (up to 24 hours based on manufacturer specifications)."*
3. **Qualify Material Composition:** Rephrase "Certified Pure 304 Stainless Steel" to *"High-grade stainless steel with laser-engraved YE SS 304 hallmark."*
4. **Obtain Owner Sign-Off:** The owner must review Gate 3 in [docs/PHASE_11_4A_IMPORT_APPROVAL_CHECKLIST.md](file:///D:/webpage/docs/PHASE_11_4A_IMPORT_APPROVAL_CHECKLIST.md) before Phase 11.4B execution.
